## About the experiment

In this demonstration, ethyl alcohol vapor mixes with the air inside a bottle. When ignited, this vapor can burn very rapidly, creating a brief visual and audible “whoosh” effect.

> [!CAUTION]
> This experiment should not be performed independently. Ethyl alcohol vapor is highly flammable, and rapid combustion inside the bottle can produce a strong flame, hot gases, or damage to the glass. Observe the experiment only as an appropriately supervised demonstration.

## How does it work?

Ethyl alcohol evaporates easily. When its vapor mixes with oxygen in the air, a combustible mixture forms.

When ignited, combustion can spread rapidly through the vapor mixture.

If the main substance is ethanol, complete combustion can be represented as:

$$
\ce{C2H5OH + 3O2 -> 2CO2 + 3H2O}
$$

During the reaction, energy is released as heat, light, and rapidly expanding hot gases.

## Why does the “whoosh” sound occur?

During rapid combustion, the gases inside the bottle heat up and expand very quickly. The fast flow of hot gases leaving through the bottle opening can create the characteristic “whoosh” sound.

> [!NOTE]
> The main cause of the effect is not the burning of liquid alcohol itself, but the rapid combustion of alcohol vapor mixed with air.
